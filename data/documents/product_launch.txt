Product Launch Brief — ARC-4 Robotic Arm

The ARC-4 robotic arm launches on November 3, 2026, targeting mid-size warehouse operators.
List price is $24,500 per unit, with a volume discount of 12% for orders of 10 or more units.
The ARC-4 has a maximum payload of 18 kilograms and a reach of 1.4 meters.
Compared to the previous ARC-3 model, the ARC-4 is 30% faster in pick-and-place cycles and uses 15% less power.
Early access customers include Meridian Logistics and Fulcrum Distribution, both of whom completed pilot programs in September 2026.
The launch event will be held in Austin, Texas, with a livestream available for remote attendees.
