Software Release Notes — Nimbus Platform v4.2

Nimbus Platform v4.2 was released on September 15, 2026, with 47 bug fixes and 12 new features.
The release reduced average API response time from 320ms to 185ms, a 42% improvement.
Database storage requirements decreased by 8% due to a new compression algorithm.
The update introduced role-based access control with 5 predefined roles: viewer, editor, manager, admin, and owner.
Maximum team size per workspace increased from 50 to 200 members.
SSO integration now supports SAML 2.0 and OpenID Connect providers.
The free tier supports up to 3 users and 1 GB of storage.
Enterprise pricing starts at $12 per user per month, billed annually.
The mobile app (iOS and Android) requires iOS 16 or Android 13 as minimum OS version.
