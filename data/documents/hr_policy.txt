Employee Time Off Policy — Effective January 2026

Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of paid time off per month, totaling 21 days per year.
Employees may carry over up to 5 unused PTO days into the following calendar year; any excess is forfeited.
New hires become eligible to use accrued PTO after completing a 90-day probationary period.
Managers must approve PTO requests at least 5 business days in advance for absences longer than 2 days.
Sick leave is tracked separately from PTO, with employees receiving 8 paid sick days per year.
Unused sick days do not carry over and are not paid out upon termination.
