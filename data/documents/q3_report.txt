Q3 2026 Financial Summary — Aurora Robotics Inc.

Aurora Robotics reported Q3 2026 revenue of $18.4 million, up 22% year-over-year.
Gross margin improved to 61%, compared to 57% in Q3 2025.
The Warehouse Automation division contributed $11.2 million of total revenue, while the Consumer division contributed $7.2 million.
Operating expenses were $9.8 million, driven mainly by a $2.1 million increase in R&D spending on the new ARC-4 robotic arm.
Net income for the quarter was $3.6 million, compared to a net loss of $0.4 million in Q3 2025.
The company ended the quarter with $42 million in cash and no outstanding debt.
Headcount grew to 214 employees, up from 178 at the end of Q2 2026.
