Warehouse Inventory Report — Q3 2026

Greenfield Distribution operates 3 warehouse facilities: Portland, Denver, and Austin.
The Portland facility has 240,000 square feet of storage and handles 12,000 orders per month.
Denver has 180,000 square feet and processes 8,500 orders per month.
Austin is the newest facility at 95,000 square feet, handling 4,200 orders per month.
Total inventory across all facilities is valued at $14.2 million.
The company shipped 187,000 units in Q3 2026, up 15% from Q2 2026.
Average order fulfillment time is 1.8 business days company-wide.
Portland averages 1.5 days, Denver averages 2.1 days, and Austin averages 2.4 days.
The return rate across all facilities is 3.2%, down from 4.1% in Q3 2025.
